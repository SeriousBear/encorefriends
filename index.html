How it started

Two friends.
Too many missed shows.

A real text thread — the one that turned into Encore.

K
What shows you going to this year so far?
J
I already have like 30 shows booked 😅
K
What?! How are you always on top of this?!
J
Always have been 😎 I book the second they drop
K
Meanwhile I blink and it's sold out
K
or I double-book myself and miss the one show I actually wanted to go to, I'm so bad at it 😩
J
lol you do this every single year
K
Well shit — let me know what shows you're going to and I'll get tickets to the ones I can make. I'd love to join.
J

K
There has to be an easier way than you sending me your whole calendar… like can't I just follow you, and when you purchase a ticket it just notifies me?
J
Yeah that would be so cool!
K
How doesn't this exist yet?!
